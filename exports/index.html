Switching to Urable

Bring your customers, pricing and job history with you

You've spent years building up your customer list and your service menu. None of it has to be retyped. Send us three exports and we'll load them into your Urable account for you.

Most shops send everything in one go, but you don't have to. Customers alone is enough to get started, and the rest can follow.

What to send

Your customer list

Start here

Names, phone numbers, emails, addresses and their vehicles. This is the one that matters most — it's what lets you start booking on day one.

Useful columns: name · phone · email · address · vehicle — whatever your system exports is fine.

Products & services

Recommended

Your service menu and pricing, including tiered prices like Coupe / Sedan / SUV. Saves rebuilding the whole price book by hand.

Useful columns: service · description · price

Job history

Optional

Past jobs with dates, vehicles, services and amounts, so your customer records show what you've already done for them.

Useful columns: date · customer · vehicle · services · total · paid

What happens next

  1. Send us the files

    Email them to your onboarding contact. Nothing needs cleaning up first.

  2. We convert and check them

    We match the format, merge duplicates, and tie vehicles to the right owners. You get a preview of what will land before anything is written.

  3. You approve, we import

    Once you're happy with the preview, it goes into your account — usually the same day.

  4. You check it over

    We'll walk the account with you. Everything imported is tagged, so anything that isn't right can be pulled back out without touching the rest.

Common questions

Do I have to stop using my current system?

No. An export is just a copy — your current system carries on exactly as before. Most shops run both for a week or two during the switch.

My data is a mess. Is that a problem?

It's normal, and no. Names and vehicles in one column, addresses in a notes field, duplicates from years of entry — we sort all of that out. A messy export is still far better than starting from scratch.

What about duplicates?

Handled. We merge records that are clearly the same person and leave the ones that only look alike — two family members sharing a home phone stay as two customers.

Is my customer data safe?

Yes. Files are used for your import and nothing else, and your data is never shared. If you'd rather send a file with pricing or notes stripped out, that's fine too.

How long does it take?

Customers are usually same-day. A full history migration with thousands of jobs takes a little longer, and we'll tell you up front.

What if something looks wrong afterwards?

Tell us. Every imported record is tagged with the batch it came from, so a bad import can be found and removed on its own without disturbing anything else in your account.

Questions about any of this? Ask your Urable onboarding contact — we'd rather answer than have you guess.
